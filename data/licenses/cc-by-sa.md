## Permissions
CC BY-SA allows copying, redistribution and adaptation for any purpose, including commercial use.

## Conditions
Attribution is required. ShareAlike: if you remix, transform or build upon the material, you must distribute your contributions under the same license (or a compatible one).

## Use in video
Because syncing music to moving images creates an adaptation, a video that uses a CC BY-SA track is itself adapted material, and the video must then be released under CC BY-SA. Commercial use is allowed, but most clients and many creators cannot license their whole video under BY-SA, so this is a condition to confirm before publishing.

## Cost
No fee under the license terms. Cost: $0 plus attribution plus ShareAlike on the resulting video.
