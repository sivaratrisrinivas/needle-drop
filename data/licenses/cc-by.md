## Permissions
CC BY lets anyone copy, redistribute, remix, transform and build upon the material for any purpose, including commercially.

## Conditions
You must give appropriate credit (creator, title, source link, license link), indicate if changes were made, and not suggest the licensor endorses you. No additional restrictions (such as DRM terms) may be applied that stop others doing what the license allows.

## Use in video
Syncing a CC BY song to video is an adaptation, which CC BY allows. A monetized YouTube video, a client ad or a film may use a CC BY track as long as the credit is given, for example in the description or end credits.

## Cost
No fee is owed to use a CC BY track under the license terms. Cost: $0 plus attribution.
