## Permissions
CC BY-NC allows copying, redistribution and adaptation for non-commercial purposes only.

## NonCommercial definition
NonCommercial means not primarily intended for or directed towards commercial advantage or monetary compensation (CC BY-NC 4.0, Section 1). A monetized YouTube video, a sponsored video, an advert or client work is usually commercial, so the NC license does not cover it.

## Use in video
Fine for a personal, non-monetized video with attribution. For commercial use you need a separate commercial license from the rights holder.

## Cost
$0 for non-commercial use with attribution. Commercial use requires a paid or negotiated license from the rights holder; the price is not part of the CC license and must be obtained from the rights holder or their licensing agent.
