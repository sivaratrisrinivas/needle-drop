## Permissions
CC BY-NC-SA allows non-commercial copying and adaptation, provided adaptations are shared under the same license.

## Use in video
A non-commercial video may use the track with attribution, and the video must be released under CC BY-NC-SA. A monetized or client video is not covered; it needs a separate commercial license from the rights holder.

## Cost
$0 for non-commercial use with attribution and ShareAlike. Commercial use requires a separate license whose price must come from the rights holder.
