## Permissions
CC BY-NC-ND is the most restrictive CC license: only non-commercial sharing of the unchanged work, with credit.

## Use in video
Syncing to video is an adaptation, which ND forbids, so the track cannot be used in any video under this license, commercial or not.

## Cost
Not usable in video under the CC license. Requires a separate sync license from the rights holder, price unknown until quoted.
