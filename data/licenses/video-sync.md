## Rule
Under CC 4.0 licenses, "where the Licensed Material is a musical work, performance, or sound recording, Adapted Material is always produced where the Licensed Material is synched in timed relation with a moving image" (Section 1(a)). CC 3.0 licenses state that synchronization of a musical work in timed relation with a moving image is considered an Adaptation.

## Consequences
NoDerivatives (ND) tracks cannot be synced to video. ShareAlike (SA) tracks make the video adapted material that must carry the same license. NonCommercial (NC) tracks cannot be used in commercial video.
