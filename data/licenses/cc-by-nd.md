## Permissions
CC BY-ND allows redistribution, including commercially, but only of the unchanged work. You may not share adapted material.

## Use in video
CC 4.0 defines Adapted Material to always include a musical work or sound recording "synched in timed relation with a moving image" (CC BY-ND 4.0, Section 1(a)); the 3.0 licenses say the same about synchronization. So putting a CC BY-ND track under a video is an adaptation the license does not allow, for any video, commercial or not.

## Cost
Not usable in video under the CC license at any price. A separate sync license from the rights holder is required.
